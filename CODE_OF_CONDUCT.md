# Code Of Conduct

DeckSoundpad contributors are expected to keep discussion professional,
specific, and focused on improving the project.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces including issues, pull requests,
discussions, release-validation threads, support conversations, and any
maintainer-managed channel used for DeckSoundpad work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be specific, constructive, and respectful when reporting bugs or reviewing
  changes.
- Assume public threads may be read by users who are debugging local audio
  devices, file paths, and input device access.
- Keep security-sensitive details, private paths, and personal information out
  of public comments.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, sustained
off-topic disruption, publishing private information, and hostile comments
about protected identity or background.

## Enforcement And Reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or restrict
participation when behavior makes collaboration unsafe or unproductive.

Do not publish private conduct reports, personal details, exploit information,
or sensitive support logs in public issue comments. Once the public project home
is finalized, report conduct concerns through the repository maintainer contact
or private reporting channel documented for the project.

Until that contact exists, open a minimal public issue asking for private
maintainer contact without including the sensitive details. If the concern also
involves a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
